## 💡 Why Project Envision?

**"Sci-fi settings are often fantastic, but conflict is always human."**

I created **Project Envision** to introduce factions that mirror **real-world scenarios** rather than just standard sci-fi tropes. The goal was to depict how distinct ideologies and survival strategies would actually clash in a resource-scarce environment.

* **The Solvaris Remnant** represents the **reality of the Industrial Complex**: A society that survives through sheer discipline, heavy manufacturing, and defensive fortification. They mirror real-world nations that prioritize stability and industry over expansion.
* **The Argent Concordat** represents the **unintended consequences of automation**: A force that was created to serve but evolved beyond control. They depict the very real fear of technology outpacing human ethics and the dangerous efficiency of a system without empathy.

This mod is an attempt to simulate these grounded, realistic dynamics within the Starsector universe.
